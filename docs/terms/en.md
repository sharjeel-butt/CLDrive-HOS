## 1. What CLDrive is

CLDrive is a **client-side cloud file manager** for HarmonyOS. It
connects to third-party cloud storage services you choose — Microsoft
OneDrive, Google Drive, and Dropbox — and lets you browse, organize,
upload, download, and manage files in those services on your behalf.

**CLDrive does not operate any servers.** It does not store your
files, has no user accounts of its own, and is not a cloud storage
provider.
